Questions, answered

Do I need to install anything?

No. AI My Task is a WhatsApp contact. You send files to a number and get your spreadsheet back in the same chat. There is no app, no website login, and we never ask for your email.

What if the AI reads something wrong?

Every extracted row is cross-checked with arithmetic (quantities × prices vs totals). Rows that don't reconcile — or that were hard to read — are marked Needs Review in your sheet, so you review a handful of flagged rows instead of re-checking everything.

What file types can I send?

Photos (JPG/PNG) and PDFs up to 6 pages. Handwritten and thermal-printed bills work too — unclear values are flagged rather than guessed.

Where is my data stored? Who can see it?

Your uploaded files are kept only long enough to process them and are automatically deleted after 30 days. Your spreadsheet is sent only to your WhatsApp number — we never create public links, and your data is never shared or sold.

Can I keep adding to the same sheet every week?

Yes — that's the point. Each time you convert, choose an existing sheet from the menu or start a new one. AI My Task appends new rows, skips duplicates, and keeps your columns consistent.
